# Neyro

## What is Neyro?
**Neyro is an AI-powered platform where you can organize your life into action-ready structure and collaborate with others to make your life easier and better quickly. It automatically organizes your thoughts, notes, and research into action-ready structure using PARA and GTD, whilst allowing you to view group activity on mobile such as screen time and past locations.**

Instead of forcing you to decide *where* everything belongs, Neyro lets you capture information instantly and uses AI to:
- classify it into **Projects, Areas, Resources, or Archive**
- surface what matters **right now**
- reduce the mental overhead of managing your life

Neyro isn’t a just notes app or a to-do list — it’s a **thinking system** that turns raw input into clarity and next actions.

## Why Neyro Exists
Modern productivity tools create more work:
- endless manual organization
- decision fatigue
- cluttered systems that decay over time
- lack of actionability

Neyro flips this model:
- **capture first, organize later**
- **AI reduces decisions**
- **actionability over perfection**

You don’t manage the system — **the system manages itself**.
